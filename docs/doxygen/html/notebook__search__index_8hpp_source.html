12/**
13 * =========================================================================================
14 * @file notebook_search_index.hpp
15 * @brief High-Performance Full-Text Search (FTS5) & Content Indexing Engine for FolioNote
16 * =========================================================================================
17 *
18 * --- ARCHITECTURE OVERVIEW ---
19 * Rather than decompressing and scanning hundreds of separate `.ink` files across disk when
20 * searching, the NotebookSearchIndex maintains an inverted FTS5 index and metadata catalog
21 * inside `notebook.db`.
22 *
23 * Key Capabilities:
24 * 1. Unified Universal Indexing:
25 * - Page titles and section names
26 * - Text boxes and sticky notes
27 * - Tags, hashtags (#todo, #important), bookmarks, and color categories
28 * - Future AI extensions: OCR handwriting transcripts & LaTeX math equations
29 *
30 * 2. Precise Spatial Locality:
31 * - Every indexed entry stores its World-Space bounding box (AABB).
32 * - Clicking a search result in the UI allows the viewport to immediately pan, zoom,
33 * and highlight the exact word, stroke cluster, or tag on the canvas.
34 *
35 * 3. Sub-Millisecond SQLite FTS5 Queries:
36 * - Full BM25 relevance ranking.
37 * - Prefix matching (e.g., "calc*" matches "calculus" and "calculator").
38 * - Automatic snippet generation with highlighted match terms (`<b>term</b>`).
39 */

64 case ContentType::MathFormula: return "Math";
