2/**
3 * =========================================================================================
4 * @file sheet_tiler.hpp
5 * @brief RNote-Style Standard Sheet Tiling & Bounding Grid Slicing Engine
6 * =========================================================================================
7 *
8 * ARCHITECTURAL ROLE:
9 * On an infinite or continuous 2D canvas, content can extend arbitrarily in world space.
10 * SheetTiler decomposes the continuous canvas into a discrete grid of standardized physical
11 * pages (e.g. A4: 210 x 297 mm, US Letter: 215.9 x 279.4 mm):
12 * 1. Evaluates 2D content AABB across all canvas strokes and polymorphic objects.
13 * 2. Computes the bounding grid of standard sheet tiles [c_min..c_max] x [r_min..r_max].
14 * 3. Supports dual traversal patterns:
15 * - Column-Major: Prints each page column from top to bottom, advancing left to right.
16 * - Row-Major: Prints each page row from left to right, advancing top to bottom.
17 * 4. Supports "Automatic Width Fit": Dynamically calculates uniform aspect-ratio scale
18 * based on the widest drawn space so wide drawings fit on a standard sheet with zero distortion.
19 * 5. Spatial binning: Maps intersecting canvas objects into their respective sheet tiles.
20 */
