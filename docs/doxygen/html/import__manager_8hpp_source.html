3 * =========================================================================================
4 * @file import_manager.hpp
5 * @brief Unified Ingestion & Migration Subsystem Facade for FolioNote
6 * =========================================================================================
7 *
8 * ARCHITECTURAL PURPOSE & DESIGN RATIONALE:
9 * Central coordination point for importing notebooks, multi-notebook libraries, and external
10 * OneNote documents into FolioNote.
11 *
12 * Coordinates:
13 * 1. OneNote Migration (`OneNoteImporter`):
14 * Ingests .one sections, .onetoc2 TOC files, .onepkg CAB archives, and OneNote folders.
15 * 2. Standalone Package Ingestion (`PackageImporter`):
16 * Ingests .notebook folders and compressed archives (.folionb.7z, .fnpack, .7z, .zip).
17 * 3. Multi-Notebook Library Ingestion (`PackageImporter`):
18 * Extracts and mounts multi-notebook library bundles (.foliolib.7z).
19 * 4. External Library Mounting:
20 * Registers external disk directories directly with `LibraryManager`.
21 * 5. Asynchronous Worker Thread Execution:
22 * Provides `std::future<...>` overloads dispatched onto `GetGlobalThreadPool()` to ensure
23 * large archive decompressions do not stutter the 120 FPS UI thread.
24 */
