2/**
3 * =========================================================================================
4 * @file app/actions/ui_action_registry.hpp
5 * @brief Central Registry, Discovery, and Dispatch Service for UI Actions
6 * =========================================================================================
7 *
8 * ARCHITECTURAL DESIGN & INVERSION OF CONTROL:
9 * --------------------------------------------
10 * UIActionRegistry serves as the single source of truth for command discovery, shortcut
11 * acceleration, and contextual action resolution across FolioNote.
12 *
13 * It decouples command producers (CanvasEngine, DocumentSession, InputManager) from command
14 * consumers (RibbonBar, ContextMenuManager, Flyout menus, Command Palette, or future UI builder).
15 *
16 * WORKING PROCESS:
17 * 1. Startup Registration: Subsystems register their capabilities as `UIAction` descriptors
18 * storing closures that mutate engine state.
19 * 2. Discovery & Introspection: Menus, toolbars, and search palettes query actions by category,
20 * tag, or context provider.
21 * 3. Execution & Dispatch: Given an action ID or keyboard shortcut chord, the registry validates
22 * predicates (`IsVisible`, `IsEnabled`) and executes the registered closure safely.
23 */
24
