2/**
3 * =========================================================================================
4 * @file core/layers/layer_compositor_manager.hpp
5 * @brief Master Coordinator for FolioNote's 3-Layer Rendering Architecture
6 * =========================================================================================
7 *
8 * ARCHITECTURAL DESIGN & INVARIANTS:
9 * ----------------------------------
10 * `LayerCompositorManager` serves as the central rendering authority in FolioNote.
11 * It encapsulates and coordinates three specialized, isolated rendering tiers:
12 *
13 * 1. Layer 1 (BakedCanvasLayer):
14 * - Caches all committed document objects (strokes, text, images, shapes, tables)
15 * into an offscreen 32-bit premultiplied ARGB raster surface (`BL_FORMAT_PRGB32`).
16 * - Re-bakes ONLY when camera bounds change or document entities are modified.
17 * - Blitted at (0, 0) directly into the destination frame context.
18 *
19 * 2. Layer 2 (LiveInteractionLayer):
20 * - Ephemeral, non-cached overlay rendered every frame on top of Layer 1.
21 * - Renders in-flight pen strokes, laser pointer trails, selection gizmos/handles,
22 * marquee boxes/lassos, text insertion carets, and live eraser reticles.
23 * - Performs ZERO heap allocations on the hot path via borrowed spans and preallocated buffers.
24 *
25 * 3. Layer 3 (EmbeddedAppLayer):
26 * - Manages interactive running applications, native controls, web view sessions,
27 * and animated video decoders.
28 * - Maps world millimeter bounds to screen pixels and invokes per-app tick/render routines.
29 *
30 * COORDINATE SYSTEM DISCIPLINE:
31 * -----------------------------
32 * - World Space (mm): Document entities and in-flight pen ink reside in physical world millimeters.
33 * - Screen Space (px): Selection gizmo frames, scale handles, rotation stems, and eraser reticles
34 * are rendered in device pixels so hit targets maintain fixed physical sizes regardless of camera zoom.
35 */
