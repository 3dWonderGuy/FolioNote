2/**
3 * =========================================================================================
4 * @file ui/overlays/overlay_manager.hpp
5 * @brief Centralized Coordinator for Floating HUDs, Diagnostic Overlays, and Modals
6 * =========================================================================================
7 *
8 * ARCHITECTURAL DESIGN & PURPOSE:
9 * --------------------------------
10 * In FolioNote, overlays and modals are decoupled from the main workspace layout.
11 * The `OverlayManager` unifies:
12 *
13 * 1. Floating HUDs:
14 * - World-to-screen tracked Audio Controller Capsule
15 * - Spotlight Action Command Palette (`ActionCommandPalette`)
16 *
17 * 2. Diagnostic Overlays:
18 * - Real-time Dev Telemetry & Frame Pacing (`DebugOverlay`)
19 * - Inking Tuning Studio (`InkingTuningOverlay`)
20 * - Toolbar Component Demo (`ToolbarDemoOverlay`)
21 *
22 * 3. Interactive Modal Dialogs:
23 * - PDF Import Placement (`PdfImportModal`)
24 * - Attachment Embed/Link Chooser Modal
25 *
26 * MATHEMATICAL & COORDINATE WORKING PROCESS:
27 * - Floating HUDs track canvas world transforms using:
28 * screenPos = canvas.transform.WorldToScreen(worldX, worldY) + canvasOrigin
29 * - Overlays render in the uppermost Z-index pass after primary viewport quads to ensure
30 * zero occlusion or input clipping by background rendering.
31 */
