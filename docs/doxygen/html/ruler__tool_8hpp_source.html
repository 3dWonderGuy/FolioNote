2/**
3 * =========================================================================================
4 * @file core/canvas_engine/tools/ruler_tool.hpp
5 * @brief Interactive Virtual Straightedge Ruler with Live Angle Tracking & Snapping
6 * =========================================================================================
7 *
8 * ARCHITECTURAL DESIGN & MATHEMATICAL PRINCIPLES:
9 * ------------------------------------------------
10 * The RulerTool provides an interactive drafting straightedge on the infinite canvas.
11 * It fulfills three core capabilities:
12 *
13 * 1. Geometric State & Spatial Placement:
14 * - Positioned at world center C = (Cx, Cy) in physical canvas millimeters (mm).
15 * - Orientation defined by angle θ (in degrees and radians).
16 * - Physical dimensions: Length L (default 240mm) and Width W (default 52mm).
17 * - Unit systems: Metric (centimeters / millimeters) and Imperial (inches / fractions).
18 *
19 * 2. Vector Straightedge Snapping Calculus:
20 * - Direction unit vector: u = (cos θ, sin θ)
21 * - Normal unit vector: v = (-sin θ, cos θ)
22 * - Top edge: A_top = C - (L/2)*u + (W/2)*v, B_top = C + (L/2)*u + (W/2)*v
23 * - Bottom edge: A_bot = C - (L/2)*u - (W/2)*v, B_bot = C + (L/2)*u - (W/2)*v
24 * - For any incoming pen coordinate P:
25 * t = (P - C) · u
26 * d_v = (P - C) · v
27 * Δ_top = |d_v - W/2|, Δ_bot = |d_v + W/2|
28 * If min(Δ_top, Δ_bot) <= snapThreshold, P is orthogonally projected onto the edge.
29 *
30 * 3. Interactive Manipulation & Presentation:
31 * - Center HUD angle dial showing digital orientation (e.g., "45.0°").
32 * - Dragging the ruler body translates C.
33 * - Dragging the rotation dial or circular end handles rotates θ.
34 * - Angle snapping: Automatically locks to 0°, 15°, 30°, 45°, 60°, 75°, 90° within ±2.0°.
35 * - Measurement tick marks:
36 * * Metric: 1cm major ticks with numerals, 5mm medium ticks, 1mm minor ticks.
37 * * Imperial: 1" major ticks with numerals, 1/2", 1/4", 1/8", 1/16" ticks.
38 * - Live stroke length gauge rendered along the active snapped edge during inking gestures.
39 */

112 /**
113 * @brief Evaluates an incoming stylus or mouse world coordinate and snaps it to the ruler

115 *
116 * Mathematical Process:
117 * 1. Evaluates direction vectors u = (cos θ, sin θ) and v = (-sin θ, cos θ).
118 * 2. Projects vector (P - C) into longitudinal offset t and transverse offset d_v.
119 * 3. Calculates distance to top straightedge (+W/2) and bottom straightedge (-W/2).
120 * 4. If within threshold and longitudinally bounded (with generous 15mm run-off),
121 * clamps transverse offset to the nearest edge and returns the snapped coordinate.
122 *
123 * @param worldPoint Input stylus coordinate in physical world millimeters.
124 * @return RulerSnapResult Snapped coordinates and edge telemetry.
125 */
