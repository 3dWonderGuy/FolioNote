2/**
3 * =========================================================================================
4 * @file core/objects/media/videos/video_container.hpp
5 * @brief Canvas Object Representing a Video Player — Local Files and Network Streams
6 * =========================================================================================
7 *
8 * ARCHITECTURAL DESIGN & INTERACTION MODEL:
9 * -----------------------------------------
10 * VideoObject is a first-class canvas citizen that owns a VideoPlayerInstance (the libVLC
11 * decode thread engine) and blits decoded BGRA32 video frames directly into the Blend2D
12 * compositeSurface each frame.
13 *
14 * Key Design Decisions:
15 * 1. Aspect Ratio ALWAYS Locked:
16 * Unlike ImageObject (which allows edge-handle squishing), VideoObject ALWAYS preserves
17 * its aspect ratio — both corner and edge handles resize proportionally. The ratio
18 * is derived from the actual decoded frame dimensions on first decode.
19 *
20 * 2. Dual Source Support:
21 * - Local physical files: MP4, MKV, WebM, MOV, AVI — copied into imports/videos/
22 * - Network streams: HTTP/HTTPS, HLS (.m3u8), RTSP — passed directly to libVLC
23 * - YouTube links: Detected by URL pattern; poster thumbnail shown, click-to-play
24 * via system browser or native player fallback
25 *
26 * 3. 3rd-Layer Decode Thread:
27 * VideoPlayerInstance decodes frames on its own background thread (libVLC's internal
28 * thread pool). The main render thread picks up new frames via HasNewFrame() + LockFrame().
29 *
30 * 4. Transport Controls Overlay:
31 * On-canvas scrubber bar (Play/Pause, timecode, seek slider, volume) rendered by
32 * Blend2D in screen-space on the compositeSurface during VideoObject::Render().
33 *
34 * Memory Model:
35 * pixelBuffer: videoW * videoH * 4 bytes (BGRA32), owned by VideoPlayerInstance
36 * player: unique_ptr<VideoPlayerInstance> — created lazily on first Play()
37 * thumbnailImg: BLImage — optional poster frame (YouTube or first-frame snapshot)
38 *
39 * Input/Output of Render():
40 * @input ctx Blend2D rendering context (screen-space coordinates)
41 * @input viewport Current camera viewport (for world→screen projection)
42 * @output Blits decoded video frame or player-card placeholder into ctx
43 */
