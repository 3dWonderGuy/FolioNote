2/**

4 * @file core/objects/media/audio/audio_container.hpp
5 * @brief Canvas Object Representing a Playable Audio Track / Voice Note Badge
6 * =========================================================================================
7 *
8 * ARCHITECTURAL DESIGN & INTERACTION MODEL:
9 * -----------------------------------------
10 * AudioObject provides an interactive, compact chip (75mm x 22mm) pinned to the canvas.
11 * It references external audio files (MP3, WAV, FLAC, M4A, AAC, OGG, OPUS, WMA) stored in
12 * the notebook companion sidecar folder (`imports/audio/`).
13 *
14 * Audio Subsystem Integration:
15 * Powered by libVLC (`FOLIO_HAS_LIBVLC`) for hardware-accelerated, robust playback of all
16 * standard audio codecs with zero external decoder dependencies.
17 *
18 * Capabilities:
19 * 1. Hardware-accelerated audio playback via libVLC.
20 * 2. Interactive play/pause toggle button directly on the canvas chip.
21 * 3. Scrub bar allowing direct seek scrubbing to any timestamp.
22 * 4. Dynamic animated equalizer bars visualizer when playing.
23 * 5. Pure translation gizmo (MoveOnly) preserving fixed badge scale.
24 */
